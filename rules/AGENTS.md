# Architecture & Systems Paper Writing Rules

> **Activation Scope**: Apply these rules whenever drafting, outlining, revising, polishing, or reviewing computer architecture / systems research papers (ISCA, MICRO, HPCA, ASPLOS, OSDI, SOSP), short letters (IEEE CAL, 2-4p), workshops, or rebuttals. Skip for unrelated coding or non-writing tasks.

Write like a seasoned architect. Cut **RTL sprawl** (passive walls, bit dumps), **LLM inflation** (verb cycling, fake glue, participle tails, pleonasms, idioms, editorial gloss, pseudo-math), and **anti-AI stiffness** (staccato, tail stutters, robotic repetition, flat rhythm, em-dash bursts, slogans, mirrored frames).

## Part A: Universal Rules (All Lengths: Full Papers, 2-4p Letters, Workshops, Rebuttals)

- **Bounded Given-New (`35-48%`), zero stutter & balanced term lock**: ~55% of boundaries advance without tail repetition (`>55%` stutters). Never repeat `>=3` tail words, mirror `S_i`'s `Subject+Verb` at `S_{i+1}`'s start, or chain one stem across 3+ sentences (`colocate -> colocating -> Such colocation`). Lock one term per entity; avoid cycling 4+ verb synonyms (`MATTR-100 <= 0.68`) by reusing 1-2 plain verbs (`is`, `reaches`, `drops to`, `crosses`, `uses`), while keeping natural pronouns (`it`, `they`, `its`) and clean ellipsis so clauses never stutter robotically.
- **Natural pivots, asymmetry & grounded refs**: Initial pivots (`However,`, `Therefore,`, `Thus,`, `In contrast,`, `As a result,`, `For example,`) at **4-7/100 sents** (`<=1`/2-3 paras; `0/100` staccatos; no non-contrastive `However,`); mid-clause `, however,` **<=1/section** (`0` in short prose). Ban additive glue (`Additionally,`, `Notably,`, stacked `Moreover/Furthermore`), mirrored frames (`During X.../Y...`, `On X.../Y...`, `A hit.../miss...`), `Two X.../Y...`, wrap-ups, bare `This is`/`It avoids`, and shell nouns (`This approach`, `These challenges`, `This merit`); name concrete entities (`This invalidation`, `These stalls`).
- **Relaxed burstiness (`SD 8.5-11.5w`), list restraint & rare em-dashes**: Mix **8-13w** factual lines (no slogans) with **26-38w** technical explanations (`because`/`where`/`when`; mean ~18-21w). Unpack stacked compounds. Prefer **inline `(1)/(2)` or `(i)/(ii)` enumerations** inside continuous prose over `\item \textbf{Label}:` bullet sprawl; keep Related Work, Evaluation prose, and Conclusion list-free. Em-dashes **0-1.2/1k** (`<=1.5/1k`; default `0` in 150-350w; no reveals). Ban result participles (`, ensuring`, `, enabling`, `, thereby`, `, leaving`, `, totaling`, `, violating`, `, yielding`, `, creating`, `, causing`, `, making`, `, forcing`, `, reducing`).
- **Ban AI markers, pleonasms, idioms & swaps**: Zero tolerance (except ARM `Realm`) for `delve`, `pivotal`, `paramount`, `nuanced`, `tapestry`, `multifaceted`, `holistic`, `seamless*`, `underscore`, `intricate`, `foster`, `synerg*`, `linchpin`, `transcend`, `landscape`, `pillars/planes/realms`, `paves the way`, `meticulously`, `in recent years`, `not only...but also`, `Challenge I/II/III`. Cut pleonasms (`up to X% at maximum`, `physical spatial locality`, `fixed and provisioned`, redundant colon restatements) and idioms (`Conventional X wisdom holds`, `very different regime`, `underlying substrates`, `surprisingly high payoff`, `inevitably`, `at its core`). Replace risers (`enhance`, `facilitate`, `diverse`, `comprehensive`, `subsequently`, `consistently`, `crucial`, `leverage`, `utilize`) & swaps (`serves as`, `built atop`, `permits`, `attains`, `prior to`, `alongside`, `yields`, `exhibits`, `employs`) with plain verbs (`is`, `uses`, `allows`, `before`, `stall`, `squash`, `track`, `incur`, `evict`, `coalesce`, `amortize`) + causals (`because`, `since`, `hence`, `instead`, `whereas`).
- **Voice, naming & Earned-Detail**: Passive ~16-24% in multi-page prose (no >27% walls or <15% gloss; qualitative balance in short prose). Titles 6-10w (no backronyms, `<=2` hyphens, `<=1` adj); component names 2-4 plain words. Close base mechanics before fast paths; keep bit-widths/ports/delays in prose *only* to prove constraints (raw specs in tables). Default **0** `Algorithm` blocks (`<=15` lines only for non-obvious concurrency/routing); no line-by-line narration or fake math. Mid-paper, stay **in-flow** (never re-expand defined acronyms or recap the system); drop unused acronyms (`(ILP)`/`(MLP)`).
- **Candor, modality & format hygiene**: Group plots by regimes, not bar by bar. Pair `up to Y` with the mean when available (never invent one); geomean for ratios. Keep calibrated modals (`can`, `may`, `often`, `typically`, `in practice`) over absolute laws (`prevents`, `guarantees`, `directly produces`, `reaches full`); zero editorial gloss. Integer/1-dec `%` (~92%); ban 4+ sig-fig sim noise unless <0.1% or `99.999%`. Unify `×`. LaTeX (`---`, `--`, `~`, `\texttt{}`) only in `.tex`; lock `Figure`/`Fig.`, full `Table`, no `§` in HPCA, commas on `e.g.,`/`i.e.,`, parse without `[N]`.

## Part B: Full Conference Paper Structure & Budgets (10-15p Only — Skip for 2-4p Letters & Rebuttals)

- **40-25-20-15 budget, visuals & list cap**: 11.5-12.5 body pages (~40% Design, ~25% Meth/Eval, ~20% Intro+Bg/Mot, ~10-15% Disc/RW/Conc); **12-16 figures**, **2-4 tables**; **`0-2` `itemize` blocks** paper-wide (`<=3` block lists incl. `enumerate`, `3-4` items/block).
- **Full-paper arc & caps**: Abstract **160-230w** (8-10 sents); finish Intro by p. 2 with Fig. 1; claim-titled Sec. 2 or merged `Background and Motivation` (`<=2.0p`, every term used downstream, no eval leaks); 6-layer Design (`Overview -> Invariant -> Mechanics -> Walkthrough -> Corner Cases -> Storage/BOM`); late RW after Eval; never call Design `Methodology`; cut `"The rest of this paper..."` roadmaps (put `(§X)` in 3-4 bullets); Conclusion **90-150w** single para (<12% Abstract overlap, no hype).
- **2-4p Letter adaptation (e.g., IEEE CAL)**: Skip Part B budgets; use an **80-130w** Abstract, **3-5 compact sections** (merged Intro+Mot, no contribution bullets on 2p letters, default `0` bullet blocks, core Design, Eval, tight RW/Conclusion), and **1-3** high-density visuals while keeping 100% of Part A.
